## RSI, Fibonacci et bougies japonaises (`engine/patterns/`, `engine/confidence.py`, `engine/exit_levels.py`, `agents/context.py`) — ad hoc, not a roadmap phase

Requested directly by the user after `George_AnalyseTechnique_Kabbaj_Synthese.md` (a 230-page French technical-analysis book, synthesized and referenced in the root-level reference material above) was added as pure documentation: "les connaissances dans ce fichier doit permettre à George d'analyser finement les courbes et de donner des conseils precis" — i.e. the book's content had to change real detection/scoring/explanation behavior, not stay a reference doc nobody reads at runtime. Two scope points confirmed directly with the user (via plan mode): RSI becomes a 4th confidence factor for **all 13 pre-existing detectors**, not just reversal patterns; and the ~12 candlestick detectors are built **in the same pass**, not deferred to a later one.

- **`engine/patterns/common.py:rsi(series, window=RSI_WINDOW)`** — Wilder's smoothing (`.ewm(alpha=1/window, adjust=False)`, not a plain rolling mean), `RSI_WINDOW=14`, `RSI_NEUTRAL_ZONE=50.0`. Handles the two degenerate cases explicitly rather than letting them fall out of the raw formula as `inf`/`NaN`: an uninterrupted rally (zero losses) → RSI pinned to `100.0`; a perfectly flat series (zero gains and zero losses) → RSI at the neutral `50.0`. Shared by every one of the now-25 detectors and by `engine/features.py:rsi_at` (the learned-model feature), not duplicated per module.
- **`engine/confidence.py`** rebalanced from the Phase 1 weights (`GEOMETRY 0.5 / VOLUME 0.25 / TREND 0.25`) to `GEOMETRY 0.4 / VOLUME 0.2 / TREND 0.2 / RSI 0.2` — `confidence_score` gained a 4th required parameter (`rsi_conf`), a breaking signature change accepted deliberately (every one of the 13 pre-existing call sites was updated in the same pass, not left on a default). `rsi_confirmation(close, at_idx, bullish)` returns `1.0` when the RSI sits on the expected side of its neutral zone for the pattern's direction, `0.0` when it contradicts it, `0.5` (neutral, same "insufficient data" convention as `trend_alignment`) when history is too short. Directly implements the synthesis's own worked counter-example (CAC 40, April 1997: a confirmed Head & Shoulders breakout that failed specifically because RSI never confirmed) — a geometrically perfect pattern with a contradicting RSI now scores meaningfully lower rather than being scored on geometry/volume/trend alone.
- **All 13 pre-existing detectors** (`reversal.py`, `continuation.py`, `breakout.py`) now compute `rsi_conf` at their confirmation bar and pass it to `confidence_score`, and store the raw value in `context["rsi_14"]` — not just used for scoring, but preserved so `agents/context.py` (below) can cite the actual number rather than recomputing or approximating it.
- **`engine/exit_levels.py:fibonacci_retracement_levels(swing_low, swing_high)`** — the 4 standard retracement ratios (38.2%/50%/61.8%/76.4%) between a swing low/high. **Deliberately informational-only**: it does not touch `propose_levels`'s existing take-profit/stop-loss formulas for any of the 13 chartist patterns — those were already validated (Phase 6) against real worked examples in the synthesis, and the synthesis's own recommendation (§7.5) was explicitly "no change needed to the existing formulas." Fibonacci levels are computed separately, only for the 5 reversal patterns whose `context` already stores an absolute swing low/high (Double Top/Bottom, Head & Shoulders + inverse, Cup & Handle — `agents/context.py`'s `_FIBONACCI_SWING_EXTRACTORS` maps each pattern type to the right two `context` keys), and surfaced to the LLM as supporting context, never as a new exit level.
- **`engine/patterns/candlestick.py`** (new, 12 detectors across 6 geometric pairs: Doji, Hammer/Hanging Man, Inverted Hammer/Shooting Star, Bullish/Bearish Engulfing, Morning/Evening Star, Piercing Line/Dark Cloud Cover) — implements the synthesis's explicit "never alone" rule (§2.1/§6: a candlestick shape by itself is not an actionable signal) as a **hard pre-filter**, separate from and in addition to the soft-scored geometry/volume/trend/RSI factors every other detector uses: each function checks the OHLC shape (body/range and shadow/body ratios, thresholds documented in the synthesis) *and* the preceding trend direction (`_preceding_trend`, a short lookback slope check), returning `[]` outright if either fails — a textbook Hammer shape after an uptrend is not a Hammer, it's discarded. This is why geometrically-identical pairs (a Hammer and a Hanging Man are the same candle shape) need two separate detector functions with two separate `pattern_type` names, rather than one function that names its output conditionally: `engine/exit_levels.py:is_bullish()`/`_LEVEL_FUNCTIONS` (Phase 6) hard-codes exactly one direction per `pattern_type` string, and preserving that existing contract was simpler and safer than changing it to accommodate context-dependent naming.
- **`engine/exit_levels.py:_candlestick_levels`** — candlesticks have no "pattern height" the way chartist patterns do (no measured-move geometry), so take-profit/stop-loss use a different, equally book-sourced rule instead: stop-loss at the signal candle's own extreme wick (with `BREAKOUT_BUFFER`), take-profit derived from a minimum **risk/reward ratio of 3** (`CANDLESTICK_RISK_REWARD_RATIO`, §5 of the synthesis) applied to the resulting risk distance — a second, independent rule from the synthesis, not an approximation of the chartist formulas. All 12 candlestick pattern types were added to `BULLISH_PATTERNS`/`BEARISH_PATTERNS`/`_LEVEL_FUNCTIONS`; skipping this would have made `engine.propose_decisions` raise `KeyError` the first time a real candlestick pattern was proposed.
- **`engine/patterns/__init__.py:DETECTORS`** now has 25 entries (13 chartist + 12 candlestick), all still one dict, one registration point, same precedent as every other detector added since Phase 1.
- **`engine/features.py`/`engine/scoring.py`** — `rsi_14` added to `NUMERIC_FEATURE_COLUMNS` and computed via `rsi_at(df, at_idx)`, but treated as a **soft/non-gating** feature at inference time (like `market_trend`), not a hard-gating one (like `atr_pct`/`relative_volume`/the moving-average features): RSI only needs 14 bars of history, far less than the SMA200 gate, so a missing value is left as `NaN` for the trained pipeline's imputer rather than aborting inference. Because `engine/scoring.py` resolves its feature columns from the trained model's own `.meta.json`, not from `NUMERIC_FEATURE_COLUMNS` directly, adding this column doesn't change or break inference against the currently-deployed model — it only takes effect after a future retrain via the existing notebook.
- **`agents/context.py:decision_context`** — the one change that actually made any of the above reach an LLM explanation, found by tracing the full chain before writing any code: `decision_context` was already dropping `pattern.context` entirely (only `pattern_type`/`detected_at`/`confidence` were ever forwarded), so RSI/Fibonacci could be computed and stored correctly and still never appear in an explanation. Now `context["pattern"]` also carries `"rsi_14"` (straight from `pattern.context`, `None` if absent — e.g. patterns detected before this change) and `"niveaux_fibonacci"` (computed on demand via `_fibonacci_context`, `None` for any pattern type without an absolute swing in its `context`, i.e. continuation patterns and all 12 candlesticks) — never a raw, unfiltered `pattern.context` passthrough, keeping the same "explicitly control what's citable" discipline as the rest of this anti-hallucination architecture. `agents/prompts/explainer_system.txt` and `agents/prompts/jane_second_opinion_system.txt` had their "éléments de confirmation" rule extended to name RSI and Fibonacci explicitly (citing the same CAC 40 1997 example for Jane's prompt) — rule 1 (never cite a number absent from context) already covers these new fields with no change needed.
- **`.github/workflows/daily_yfinance_import.yml`** gained two more steps (`engine.run_detection` then `engine.propose_decisions`) after the existing price/fundamentals ingestion steps — closing a real gap surfaced mid-planning when the user asked for the ability to "submit a price history and get an analysis": clarified via AskUserQuestion that the actual need wasn't a new submission endpoint (the 102 tracked securities' history is already in the database), but daily automation of the analysis that already exists and, until this change, only ever ran manually. No new endpoint or UI was built as a result — every existing screen (Univers/Paniers) now shows freshly-detected patterns and freshly-proposed decisions each morning without any manual trigger.
- **Out of scope for this pass** (documented rather than silently dropped, same discipline as every other ad hoc feature above): no arbitrary/off-universe curve submission endpoint; Elliott wave detection (the synthesis itself flags wave identification as too interpretation-dependent for a reliable automatic detector); pair trading; a Livermore-style pyramiding/reinforcement mechanic (George never reinforces an existing decision — a bigger philosophy change than this pass); actually retraining the XGBoost model with the new `rsi_14` feature (the feature is wired and ready, retraining stays the existing manual notebook step).
- **Verified**: `pytest -q` — 254 passed, 13 skipped (skips are the risk/reward-ratio test correctly skipping the 13 non-candlestick pattern types). Verified live against the real production Supabase database: `engine.run_detection` ran cleanly across the full ~102-security universe with no crashes, produced 275 real pattern detections including genuine candlestick matches; `engine.propose_decisions` produced 17 real proposals with no `KeyError` on any candlestick-sourced decision; a direct `decision_context()` call against a real VIRI.PA Double Bottom detection confirmed real RSI and Fibonacci values were correctly surfaced.

## Auto-learn: automated confidence-model retraining (`engine/train_confidence_model.py`) — ad hoc, not a roadmap phase

Requested directly by the user ("create a process the LLM can auto learn") — clarified via `AskUserQuestion` into what it actually maps onto in this codebase: there is no fine-tunable LLM in George (Mistral is a stateless hosted API call), so the only real "learned" component is the XGBoost confidence model (Phase 2). Two scope points confirmed directly with the user: automate *this* model's retraining, and keep a human validation gate before a retrained model ever reaches production (a bad retrain would otherwise silently degrade real Achat/Alléger recommendations) — same "propose, never auto-execute" discipline already used for Achat/Alléger decisions themselves.

- **A real, significant discovery made before building anything, changing the whole plan**: the XGBoost model has likely *never actually run in the deployed/scheduled system*. `engine/models/*.joblib` was fully gitignored — it only ever existed on this local dev machine (leftover from earlier manual notebook runs), never in git, never on Render, and never in the GitHub Actions runner (fresh checkout every run). Since `engine.run_detection` only ever executes via that scheduled workflow (or manually), and the workflow had no model file to load, `engine.scoring.load_latest_model()` always returned `(None, None)` there — every *scheduled* production detection run has always silently fallen back to the plain heuristic score; the learned model only ever activated when someone (in this session, Claude) ran detection locally by hand. Confirmed via `AskUserQuestion`: fix by tracking the *promoted* model in git (not a new storage dependency) so the workflow's checkout actually has it.
- **Another real, concrete gap found while first trying to retrain**: `python -m engine.build_training_set` against production returned **0 backtestable samples** — every pattern in `patterns_detected` had been detected within the last 2-3 trading sessions (the RSI/candlestick/price_history_long work in this same session is only days old), nowhere near `TRAINING_HORIZON` (10 sessions) of hindsight needed to know a pattern's outcome. Not a bug — retraining today would just reproduce the same 800-sample synthetic placeholder the currently-deployed model was trained on (`dataset_source: "synthétique (démonstration)"`, accuracy 0.56 / ROC AUC 0.57 — barely above chance). Real training data becomes available only once enough patterns age past 10 sessions (~mid-August from the earliest current detections) — the automated pipeline below is built now so it's *ready* the moment that happens, not run manually today for a result with no real value yet.
- **`engine/train_confidence_model.py`** ports `engine/notebooks/train_confidence_model.ipynb`'s training logic (dataset loading with synthetic fallback, sklearn `ColumnTransformer`+`XGBClassifier` pipeline, train/test split, accuracy/ROC AUC) into an importable, automatable module — the notebook now imports `load_dataset`/`CANDIDATES_DIR` from here rather than duplicating the logic, so notebook and CI stay identical by construction. `train_candidate()` **always saves to `engine/models/candidates/`**, never directly to `engine/models/` (the live location `engine/scoring.py:latest_model_path` actually reads — its glob doesn't recurse into subdirectories, so a candidate is structurally invisible to production until explicitly promoted) — and returns a comparison report (candidate metrics vs. the currently-live model's, read from its `.meta.json`) rather than just the candidate's own numbers, so a promotion decision is never made on the candidate's score in isolation.
- **A second real bug caught by actually executing the (refactored) notebook end-to-end**, not assumed: `KeyError: "['rsi_14'] not in index"` — the synthetic-dataset generator predates `rsi_14` being added to `NUMERIC_FEATURE_COLUMNS` (Kabbaj synthesis pass) and was never updated, so `dataset[FEATURE_COLUMNS]` failed the moment RSI became a required feature column. This had been silently broken since that RSI pass — nobody had re-run the notebook since. Fixed by adding a plausible `rsi_14` column to `make_synthetic_dataset` (centered above/below the neutral zone by pattern direction, same convention as the other pre-biased synthetic features); verified by actually executing the notebook via `jupyter nbconvert --to notebook --execute` (not just importing the module) and confirming the new tests catch this class of regression directly (`test_make_synthetic_dataset_has_every_feature_column`, iterating `FEATURE_COLUMNS` rather than hardcoding column names, so a future feature addition can't silently repeat this).
- **`engine/promote_candidate_model.py`** is the human validation gate itself: `python -m engine.promote_candidate_model [stamp]` copies (never moves — the candidate stays available for audit) a candidate's `.joblib`+`.meta.json` from `candidates/` into `engine/models/`, making it live at the next `engine.run_detection`. Deliberately does **not** `git add`/commit/push itself — same "confirm before an irreversible-ish shared-state action" discipline as the rest of this project; the human runs it, reviews the result, then commits/pushes explicitly.
- **`engine/scoring.py:_latest_model_path` renamed to `latest_model_path`** (dropped the leading underscore) — it's now a genuinely shared utility between `engine/scoring.py` (production inference) and `engine/train_confidence_model.py` (the comparison report needs to know exactly which file inference would actually load), not a private implementation detail of one module. Deliberately not duplicated the way `ohlcv.py`'s tiny `_to_aware` is elsewhere in this codebase — this one carries real glob/sort logic worth keeping as one source of truth rather than risking drift between two copies.
- **`.gitignore`** changed from blanket-ignoring `engine/models/` to ignoring only `engine/models/candidates/` — the promoted model files directly under `engine/models/` are now tracked, which is precisely what makes them reach the GitHub Actions checkout. The current local models (previously never committed) were promoted and committed as this pass's starting point.
- **`weekly_confidence_model_training.yml`** (split out from the price/detection workflow once that one went twice-weekly — see the scheduling bullet above) runs `engine.build_training_set` then `engine.train_confidence_model`, followed by `actions/upload-artifact@v4` uploading `engine/models/candidates/` (30-day retention) — since that directory is gitignored, the ephemeral GitHub Actions runner would otherwise lose the candidate the moment the job ends. The comparison report (candidate vs. live metrics) prints to the job's own log, reviewable from the Actions tab without needing to download anything; only actually promoting requires downloading the artifact and running `engine.promote_candidate_model` locally (or wherever `DATABASE_URL`-equivalent access isn't even needed for this step — promotion touches only local files).
- **Verified**: new `tests/test_train_confidence_model.py` (8 tests, pure — isolates `scoring.MODELS_DIR`/`MODEL_GLOB` and `train_confidence_model.CANDIDATES_DIR` under `tmp_path`, same monkeypatch-module-constants precedent as `tests/test_scoring.py`): synthetic dataset has every `FEATURE_COLUMNS` entry, real-vs-synthetic dataset loading, candidate always saved under `candidates/` never the live dir, comparison report correct with/without an existing live model, promotion copies correctly, promotion raises on a missing/unknown candidate. Full suite (`pytest -q`, 267 passed) green. Notebook re-executed end-to-end via `jupyter nbconvert --to notebook --execute` after the refactor (this is what caught the `rsi_14` bug above). `python -m engine.train_confidence_model` → `python -m engine.promote_candidate_model` run live end-to-end on this machine, producing a real (if still synthetic-data-trained) improvement over the previous model (ROC AUC 0.571 → 0.633).

## MACD, Bollinger Bands & Stochastic (`engine/patterns/common.py`, `engine/confidence.py`, `engine/features.py`, `engine/exit_levels.py`, `agents/context.py`, `mobile/lib/core/indicators/`, `mobile/lib/features/univers/widgets/candlestick_chart.dart`) — ad hoc, not a roadmap phase

Requested directly by the user, in the same spirit as the earlier RSI/Fibonacci/candlestick pass (see "RSI, Fibonacci et bougies japonaises" above): George_AnalyseTechnique_Kabbaj_Synthese.md §4 documents MACD, Bollinger Bands and the stochastic oscillator as concepts from the book that were, until this pass, reference-only knowledge — nowhere in `engine/`. This closes that gap for all three, following each indicator's own documented use case in the synthesis rather than bolting all three onto the same mechanism uniformly.

- **`engine/patterns/common.py`** gained `ema`/`macd`/`bollinger_bands`/`stochastic_oscillator`, pure functions on top of the existing `sma`/`rsi` (same `min_periods`-gated-`NaN` convention throughout). `macd` returns `(macd_line, signal_line, histogram)` — EMA(12)-EMA(26), its own EMA(9), and their difference. `bollinger_bands` returns `(mid, upper, lower)` — SMA(20) ± 2 population standard deviations (`ddof=0`). `stochastic_oscillator` returns `(%K, %D)` on a 14/3 window, with the same `replace(0, np.nan)` zero-denominator guard already used by `rsi` for a flat range.
- **`engine/confidence.py:confidence_score`** rebalanced again (`GEOMETRY 0.4/VOLUME 0.2/TREND 0.2/RSI 0.2` → `GEOMETRY 0.3/VOLUME 0.15/TREND 0.15/RSI 0.15/MACD 0.15/STOCH 0.10`) and gained two new sub-scores. `macd_confirmation(close, at_idx, bullish)` mirrors `rsi_confirmation` exactly, but against the MACD line's zero crossing — the synthesis's own framing (§4: *"la ligne du zéro = équivalent de la zone de neutralité du RSI"*) justifies applying it uniformly to all 25 detectors, same reasoning already used to justify RSI's uniform application. `stochastic_confirmation(high, low, close, at_idx, bullish)` is deliberately **not** applied uniformly: the synthesis only ever documents the stochastic in combination with candlesticks (§2.7/§4 — exiting overbought/oversold *plus* a candle pattern), never as a general chartist-pattern filter, so `confidence_score` takes it as an **optional** 6th parameter (`stoch_conf: float | None = None`). When omitted (all 13 classic chartist detectors), its weight is redistributed proportionally across the other five (`scale = 1 / (1 - STOCH_WEIGHT)`) rather than silently docking every non-candlestick pattern for a dimension that was never documented as applying to it — same "`None` over a fabricated 0" philosophy as `engine/george_index.py`.
- **All 25 detectors** (`reversal.py`/`continuation.py`/`breakout.py`, 11 call sites; `candlestick.py`'s shared `_score_and_match`) now compute `macd_conf` and pass it as `confidence_score`'s 5th argument, storing the raw MACD line value in `context["macd_line"]` (same "preserve the real number for the explanation" precedent as `rsi_14`). Only `candlestick.py` additionally computes `stoch_conf` and stores `context["stochastic_confirmation"]` — the 13 chartist detectors never call `stochastic_confirmation` at all, not just leave it at a default.
- **`engine/features.py`/`engine/scoring.py`** — `macd_hist_pct` (MACD histogram, normalized by close price like `atr_pct`), `bb_percent_b` (price's position within the Bollinger bands, 0 at the lower band/1 at the upper), and `stochastic_k` (%K, 0-100) added to `NUMERIC_FEATURE_COLUMNS`, all three treated as soft/non-gating features exactly like `rsi_14` (imputed via `NaN` if unavailable, never blocking inference) — none needs more than a few dozen sessions, far short of the SMA200 gate. `engine/train_confidence_model.py:make_synthetic_dataset` updated in the same pass (not left to repeat the exact `KeyError: "['rsi_14'] not in index"` bug already documented and fixed once above) — plausible directionally-biased synthetic values added for all three alongside `rsi_14`.
- **`engine/exit_levels.py:bollinger_band_levels(df, at_idx)`** — informational only, same non-replacing role as `fibonacci_retracement_levels` (the synthesis §4 cites Bollinger for adjusting a stop to real volatility, not for a new measured-move formula). Unlike Fibonacci (which reads its swing points from a pattern's already-stored `context`), Bollinger needs the full price history, which no `PatternDetected.context` keeps — so it's computed once in `engine/run_detection.py:run` (which already loads the full `price_history_long` `df` per symbol to detect against) and stored as `context["niveaux_bollinger"]`, the same enrichment point already used for `confidence_source`, rather than recomputed later without the price history available.
- **`agents/context.py:decision_context`** forwards `macd_line`, `niveaux_bollinger` (straight passthrough, `None` if absent, same discipline as `rsi_14`), and the two new sub-scores (`scores.macd`, `scores.stochastique`) — `scores.stochastique` is `None` for the 13 chartist pattern types (never fabricated, matching what's actually in `context`). `ponderations` normally forwards `_CONFIDENCE_WEIGHTS` (now including `macd`) but only adds a `stochastique` key when `pattern.pattern_type` is one of the 12 candlestick types (`_CANDLESTICK_PATTERN_TYPES`) — showing `STOCH_WEIGHT` unconditionally would have implied every pattern's score includes a 10% stochastic contribution, which is false for the 13 chartist ones (their weight is redistributed, not stochastic-driven).
- **Verified**: new `tests/test_confidence.py` (weight-sum sanity check, `confidence_score` with/without `stoch_conf` at both extremes, `macd_confirmation`/`stochastic_confirmation` on synthetic rallies/selloffs and before their window warms up) plus new cases in `tests/test_features.py` (`macd_hist_pct`/`bb_percent_b`/`stochastic_k`) and `tests/test_exit_levels.py` (`bollinger_band_levels` ordering). Three pre-existing `tests/test_agents.py` cases updated for the new `scores`/`ponderations` shape (plus one new case confirming the candlestick-only `stochastique` weight), and `engine/train_confidence_model.py`'s synthetic dataset extended. Full non-Docker suite green (`pytest -q`, 184 passed) after fixing a real bug caught by that same run: `agents/context.py:decision_context` computed a per-pattern-type `weights` dict but the returned `context["pattern"]["ponderations"]` still referenced the unconditional `_CONFIDENCE_WEIGHTS` constant directly — caught by the new candlestick-weight test, not by inspection.
- **Mobile chart** (`mobile/lib/core/indicators/technical_indicators.dart`, `mobile/lib/features/univers/widgets/candlestick_chart.dart`) — requested directly by the user: show MACD/Bollinger/Stochastic on the 10-year chart popup (`decision_detail_screen.dart:_showChartPopup`), not just compute them server-side. `technical_indicators.dart` is a self-contained Dart port of `engine/patterns/common.py`'s `ema`/`macd`/`bollinger_bands`/`stochastic_oscillator` — computed client-side from the popup's already-loaded full price history (`securityFullPricesProvider`), no new API endpoint needed. `CandlestickChart` gained a `showIndicators` flag (default `false`, so the small 250-session inline chart in `DecisionDetailScreen` is unaffected): when `true`, Bollinger bands overlay the price panel and two new stacked sub-panels render MACD (histogram + line + signal, symmetric zero-centered scale) and the stochastic oscillator (fixed 0-100 scale, dashed 20/80 reference lines).
- **A real, significant clarity bug caught by actually rendering the widget** (via a temporary `matchesGoldenFile` preview with synthetic 600-bar data — no Android emulator needed for this check), not assumed from reading the code: cramming ~2500 daily candles (10 years) into a ~350px-wide popup was correctly identified upfront as illegible, so the canvas width was decoupled from the viewport (`contentWidth = candles.length * minCandleWidth`, wrapped in `InteractiveViewer(constrained: false)`, auto-scrolled to the most recent séances on open via a `TransformationController`) — but the *first* rendered preview revealed a second, non-obvious bug the code review alone hadn't caught: the price/MACD panels' vertical scale was computed from the **entire** 10-year series' min/max, not the currently visible window, so scrolling to a period whose prices/histogram aren't near the all-time extreme renders it squeezed into a small fraction of the panel — real screenshot showed candles occupying under a third of their panel's height with a large dead zone below. Fixed by listening to the `TransformationController` (`AnimatedBuilder`) and recomputing a `visibleRange` (via `Matrix4.inverted` + `MatrixUtils.transformPoint` on the viewport corners) on every pan/zoom frame, then scoping `_CandlestickPainter`/`_MacdPainter`'s min/max computation to that visible slice — all candles/series are still *drawn* (`InteractiveViewer` clips what's on-screen), only the axis *scale* changes. The stochastic panel needed no such fix — its 0-100 scale is fixed by definition, not derived from the data. Re-rendered the same golden preview after the fix and confirmed (both visually and by measuring non-background pixel rows in the PNG with Pillow) that all three panels now fill their allotted height with no dead zones.
- **Verified**: new `mobile/test/core/indicators/technical_indicators_test.dart` (9 cases — null-before-warmup and directional correctness on synthetic rallies/selloffs, mirroring `tests/test_confidence.py`/`tests/test_features.py`'s style on the backend). `flutter analyze` clean, `flutter test` (15 tests) green. The temporary golden-preview test used to catch and confirm the fix above was deleted afterward (a one-off visual check, not a maintained regression golden — this codebase has no other golden-file precedent to extend).
- **A second real bug found on a physical device, after the golden-preview checks above**: the "Bollinger (20, 2)"/"MACD (12, 26, 9)"/"Stochastique (14, 3)" panel labels were drawn *inside* each `CustomPainter`'s canvas at a fixed `Offset(4, 2)` — harmless in the synthetic preview (which never scrolled), but on a real 10-year history the chart opens scrolled to the *most recent* end of a canvas that starts at the *oldest* data, so that fixed offset sat off-screen to the left and no label was ever visible. Fixed by moving the three labels out of the `CustomPainter`s entirely into a `Stack` of `Positioned` widgets layered *above* the `InteractiveViewer` (a `_PanelLabel` widget with a semi-opaque background chip for legibility over dense chart content) — pinned to the viewport's corners, so they stay visible regardless of pan/zoom instead of moving with the scrollable content.

## 35 new pattern detectors from George_Strategies_Images_Synthese.md (`engine/patterns/reversal.py`, `continuation.py`, `candlestick.py`, `crossover.py` (new), `smart_money.py` (new), `engine/exit_levels.py`) — ad hoc, not a roadmap phase

Requested directly by the user, following the exact same synthesis-then-integration precedent as the Kabbaj book (RSI/Fibonacci/candlesticks) and the MACD/Bollinger/Stochastic pass: `George_Strategies_Images_Synthese.md` catalogued 35 concepts absent from the 25 existing detectors, drawn from 16 social-media reference images of uneven reliability (see that document's own §3 reservations). Given the scale (~35 new detectors, not the 2-3 of prior additions) and the source's documented unreliability, this pass used a 5-agent parallel build-then-independent-review workflow rather than a single sequential implementation — each agent owned one pattern family end-to-end (its own detector code + its own test file), then a second independent agent re-read the actual diff and re-ran the tests itself rather than trusting the first agent's self-report.

- **6 classic reversal patterns** (`engine/patterns/reversal.py`): Triple Top/Bottom (direct 3-touch extension of the existing Double Top/Bottom), Rounding Top/Bottom (quadratic-regression curve fit with an R² and vertex-centering check — genuinely different geometry from the file's existing Cup & Handle, not a renamed version of it), Diamond Top/Bottom (bar-range expansion-then-contraction). **Known, disclosed simplification**: Diamond's geometry is measured as per-bar high-low amplitude, not swing-high/low envelope structure (the textbook definition) — the review agent flagged this explicitly as a documented heuristic, not a hidden defect; revisit if Diamond's real-world hit rate looks off.
- **6 classic continuation patterns** (`engine/patterns/continuation.py`): Symmetrical Triangle (opposite-slope converging trendlines, distinct from the file's existing Ascending/Descending Triangle and from Wedge), Rectangle (two-sided tested horizontal range), Bull/Bear Pennant (a Flag-style flagpole followed by a converging, not parallel, consolidation) — bullish/bearish variants throughout.
- **13 additional candlestick patterns** (`engine/patterns/candlestick.py`): Harami, Three White Soldiers/Three Black Crows, Kicker, Dragonfly Doji, Marubozu, Inside/Outside Bar. **A real bug was caught by the review agent and fixed before merge**: `_three_soldiers_or_crows` never actually checked that the three candles had "long" bodies (the entire point of the pattern's name) and only bounded one of the two shadows — three razor-thin-bodied candles with a large wick on the unconstrained side passed every existing check and were reported as "Three White Soldiers." Fixed with a new `SOLDIER_MIN_BODY_RATIO` (body ≥ 60% of range) plus bounding both shadows, not just the trailing one; a regression test reproducing the exact false positive was added (`tests/test_candlestick_extended.py::test_three_white_soldiers_no_match_on_thin_bodies_with_opposite_wick`).
- **Golden Cross / Death Cross** (`engine/patterns/crossover.py`, new file): the first detector family that isn't OHLC-candle geometry — an SMA50/SMA200 crossover *event* (fires only on the exact bar of the cross, not for every subsequent bar the alignment persists).
- **8 Smart Money Concepts** (`engine/patterns/smart_money.py`, new file): Fair Value Gap (the standard, unambiguous 3-candle ICT non-overlap definition), Order Block (last opposite-colored candle before a confirmed impulsive move, fires on retest), Break of Structure (close beyond the most recent significant swing extremum, distinct from the existing Breakout/Breakdown's multi-touch-tested-level approach). **Breakaway Gap is a deliberate, documented departure from the source material**: the two source images disagreed with each other on the FVG-vs-BAG boundary, so this implementation uses the classical (pre-ICT) definition instead — a real price gap (not just 3-candle non-overlap) breaking out of an already-tested consolidation range, with volume confirmation — explicitly not an attempt to reverse-engineer the ambiguous screenshots.
- **`engine/exit_levels.py`** gained all 35 take-profit/stop-loss formulas (measured-move or structural-level, matching each pattern's own geometry) and their bullish/bearish classification — written centrally here (not scattered into the detector files) to preserve the existing one-file-per-concern layout, using the exact formula code each pattern-family agent already drafted against its own geometry.
